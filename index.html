Everything in one calm place

Today, Food, Workout and Sleep, each with its own plan and history. No more checking five apps to decide whether today is a rest day.

Readiness, Sleep & Stress

Three scores every morning from your HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and recent training, with a plain-English read on what they mean for today.

A plan that fits your day

Workout intensity matched to how recovered you are, a time that works around your calendar, and a bedtime that gets you the sleep you need.

Guided workouts

Follow today’s workout set by set with rest timers. Don’t like an exercise? Swap it for one of 150+ from the library, matched to your equipment.

Food by photo or barcode

Snap a meal and Orbit estimates the calories and macros, or scan a barcode. Group items into meals, save favorites and look back at any day.

Meal plans, recipes & groceries

Orbit plans up to a week of meals that hit your calories and protein, each with a recipe, and builds your grocery list sorted by aisle.

A coach that’s always there

Tap the Orbit button on any screen to ask why you’re tired, what to eat tonight or whether to train. It can change your plan for you.

Insights only you have

Orbit learns your patterns over weeks: what late meals do to your sleep, how workouts move your HRV, and your best and worst nights.

Groups & challenges

A weekly leaderboard with friends, plus short challenges for steps, sleep, recovery or workouts. You choose what’s shared.

Cycle-aware

Track your cycle and see how each phase can affect mood, energy, cravings, training and sleep, right on your Today screen.

Private by design

Your health data is yours. Orbit only uses it to build your plan.

  • Never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • Calendar stays on your phone. Event details are never uploaded.
  • Insights are worked out on your phone, and your meal plan and grocery list stay there too.
